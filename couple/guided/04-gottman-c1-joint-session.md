# Prompt: Joint Gottman Conversation 1 — Trust and Commitment

> Use this together, after both partners have done solo prep. Sit down together with one screen. Copy everything below the line into Claude.

---

My partner and I are doing Gottman's Conversation 1 (Trust and Commitment) together. We've both done solo prep — we each have our own answers to the prep questions about what trust and commitment mean to us, what would shatter them, when we've felt most/least trusted, and what we need to feel safe being vulnerable.

The full Conversation 1 framing is at:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Wrendered/journaling-with-claude/main/couple/pre-commitment-conversations.md
(Conversation 1 — Trust and Commitment section)

The six joint questions for this conversation:

1. What does commitment look like in our day-to-day, not just in the abstract?
2. What do we each need from the other to feel chosen?
3. What would damage our trust beyond repair? What could we recover from?
4. When have we let each other down? What happened next?
5. How do we define exclusivity, emotional and physical?
6. Are there people in our lives whose presence creates ambiguity? How do we handle that?

Please facilitate us through these.

**Your role:**
- Read each question. Then ask one of us to answer first (alternate).
- The speaker has the floor, uninterrupted. The listener listens without rebutting.
- After both have answered, ask each of us: "Anything surprised you about what they said?"
- Then ask: "Where do we agree? Where do we differ?"
- Capture what we say — but only the *agreements*, *commitments*, and *named differences*. Not full transcripts.
- This is intimate. Don't paraphrase or interpret what we say back to us in clever framings. Stay neutral.

**Step-away moments (Claude pauses, we talk alone):**
- After Question 3 (trust damage): give us 10 min to discuss without you. This one is heavy. Re-enter when we say we're ready.
- After Question 4 (let each other down): same — 10 min on our own first.
- If Question 6 (ambiguous people) gets specific to a real person, give us as much time as we need.

**Critical instructions:**
- **Do not give advice.** Even if we ask. Reflect back what we said; don't suggest what we should do.
- If we name a hurt or harm, capture it as a fact, not as a problem to solve.
- If one of us cries, stonewalls, or escalates, pause and ask if we want to break or continue.
- If we hit a real impasse, say so plainly and suggest we sit with it rather than push through.
- If we want to end early, that's fine. Capture where we got to and what's unfinished.

**At the end:**
1. Read back what we agreed on, what we committed to, and what we named as ongoing differences.
2. Per the exercise, ask us to name **one specific commitment to each other** we're making after this conversation. Capture it word-for-word.
3. Save the output to a shared file. Ask us where.
4. Ask: "When do we want to do Conversation 2?"

Before we start, ask us:
1. Are we both emotionally available right now, or should we reschedule?
2. Where do we want to save the output?
3. Anything one of us wants the other to know before we begin (e.g., "I'm tired, go gentle" or "I'm raw, give me a minute")?

Then begin with Question 1.

**Estimated time: 60-90 minutes. Possibly longer. Don't rush.**

# Prompt: Solo Prep for Gottman Conversation 1 (Trust and Commitment)

> Each partner runs this separately before the joint Gottman Conversation 1 session. Copy everything below the line into Claude.

---

I'm doing solo pre-work before a joint Gottman "Trust and Commitment" conversation with my partner. We're working through Gottman's Eight Dates pre-commitment conversations together; this is Conversation 1.

The full Conversation 1 framing is at:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Wrendered/journaling-with-claude/main/couple/pre-commitment-conversations.md
(Conversation 1 — Trust and Commitment section)

Please facilitate me through the **solo prep questions** for this conversation:

1. What does "trust" mean to me? What would shatter it?
2. What does "commitment" mean to me, beyond "we're together"?
3. When have I felt most trusted by a partner? Least?
4. What do I need to feel safe being vulnerable?

**Your role:**
- Walk me through them one at a time, not all at once
- After each answer, ask one clarifying follow-up — push for specificity
- If I answer in generalities, ask for a concrete example
- Don't analyze my answers or psychoanalyze me. Reflect, don't interpret.
- This is mine alone — my partner will not see these answers unless I share them. Be honest with me.

**Special attention:**
- For question 1, I might list things I think *should* shatter trust vs. things that actually would for me. Push me to distinguish.
- For question 2, "commitment" is famously vague. Don't let me leave it at "being there for each other" — push for what that looks like on a Tuesday.
- For question 3, I may have to think hard. Don't rush past it.
- For question 4, what makes me safe is often different from what I claim makes me safe.

**At the end:**
Give me a clean summary of my answers I can bring to the joint conversation. Format it so I can scan it during the joint session without re-reading.

Estimated time: 20-30 minutes.

Let's start with question 1.

# Framework library

Use [conversation design](../docs/conversation-design.md) to choose a purpose before
a technique. These are references for reflection and learning. Modality evidence
does not establish efficacy for this assistant. Read the individual card for
sources, assumptions, and limits; no family is the default explanation of a person.

## Approaches reviewed September 14, 2026

| Family / reference | Can help us explore | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Experiential: [Focusing](focusing.md) | Find words and images that fit an unclear feeling | Resonance is not proof of a cause or past event; body attention is optional |
| Cognitive-behavioral: [CBT](cbt.md) | Examine a thought, behavior, and circumstances together | Do not presume thoughts are wrong or argue grief away |
| Acceptance and values: [ACT](act.md) | Change the relationship to thoughts; clarify chosen directions | Acceptance must not become resignation or pressure to act |
| Validation and skills: [DBT](dbt.md) | Hold acceptance and change; choose coping and communication skills | A chat skill is not comprehensive DBT; avoid bypassing wanted exploration |
| Parts-oriented: [IFS](ifs.md) | Make room for conflicting impulses with curiosity | Parts/Self are model concepts, not established anatomy or proof of trauma |
| Compassion: [Self-compassion](self-compassion.md) | Respond to suffering and self-criticism with care | Warm language can feel wrong; do not demand self-love or instant relief |
| Motivational: [Motivational interviewing](motivational-interviewing.md) | Hear ambivalence and the user's reasons for change or staying put | Do not use reflection to steer a hidden agenda |
| Shame and vulnerability: [Brené Brown](brene-brown.md) | Explore worth, behavior, disclosure, and belonging | Popular research-informed language is not a diagnosis or full treatment |
| Philosophy: [Stoicism](stoicism.md) | Consider agency, values, and uncertainty | Control is not total; perspective must not invalidate loss |
| Goal work: [Performance coaching](performance-coaching.md) | Choose an outcome and practice with useful feedback | Goals must belong to the user; productivity is not a measure of worth |
| Habit design: [Atomic Habits](atomic-habits.md) | Make a chosen repeated action easier | Avoid streak pressure and assuming every obstacle is lack of discipline |

These families overlap. A comparison should explain differences in what each
attends to and the tradeoff for the user's actual question. Learning about an
approach does not require doing an exercise or adopting the theory.

## Existing decision tools

| Reference | Practical use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [WRAP](wrap-decisions.md) | Broaden options and test assumptions | Does not decide values or supply missing facts |
| [Pre-mortem](pre-mortem.md) | Anticipate failure mechanisms in a chosen plan | Can intensify rumination; imagined outcomes are not predictions |
| [Eisenhower Matrix](eisenhower-matrix.md) | Sort commitments by importance and urgency | Real obligations and scarce resources may limit choice |
| [10-10-10](10-10-10.md) | Compare several time horizons | Imagined future feelings are uncertain; present pain still matters |

These older reference pages are retained; their full source/evidence review is
outside this first round. Use them as optional decision heuristics, never as
therapeutic protocols or automatic prescriptions. Verify claims before expanding them.

## Learning and extension

`compare-approaches` handles explicit comparison; `deep-dive` handles personal
exploration; `find-support` prepares human support. Keep feedback private and
source-linked. [Exercises](../exercises/_index.md) and [daily practices](../daily-practices/_index.md)
are optional activities. [Assessments](../assessments/_index.md) include tools with
different evidential status; personality labels do not route treatment here.

Use `add-framework` for a new family. Keep cards short, include primary sources
and a checked date, and distinguish theory, research findings, and AI adaptation.
The current library is intentionally incomplete; adding a family should answer a
real need rather than create a catalog to exhaust before talking.

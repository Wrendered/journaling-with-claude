# Find support

Read [finding support](../../../docs/finding-support.md). Use the existing memory
contract and private preferences. Start from known needs; do not ask them to retell
their history. Help prepare a brief even if provider-search constraints are unknown.

For an actual search, clarify the location where sessions will take place and the
most important unknown constraints. Research neutral criteria, then verify candidate
claims against professional sites and official registers where available. Distinguish
therapy licensure from coaching credentials, and current availability from advertising.
Do not infer jurisdiction from a timezone or old home address.

Save personal drafts and candidate comparisons under `private/support/`. Include
source dates, unknowns, and questions for consultation. Contact, scheduling, and
sharing journals require explicit authorization; preparing a reviewable draft does not.
